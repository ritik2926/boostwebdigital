# References

## Design ambition: Awwards-tier

The bar for this site is Awwards-caliber execution — the level of craft, motion, and composition that gets a site nominated, not a competent template. That means every section earns its place through rhythm and storytelling, not decoration for its own sake. This is an explicit, standing target, not a stretch goal to revisit later.

## Quality-bar references — **not** layouts to copy or imitate

- **https://delucks.com/en** — storytelling, typography, motion
- **https://grainient.supply/** — composition, visual rhythm
- **https://orionix.framer.website/** — premium interactions
- **https://digitz.fr/** — added reference, study for motion/interaction craft
- **https://trionn.com/** — added reference, study for typography/composition

Study why these feel exceptional: rhythm, hierarchy, spacing, pacing, motion, emotional response. The goal is to exceed the experience these sites deliver while staying practical and performant for a bootstrapped, non-programmer-maintained site — not to reproduce their specific layouts.
