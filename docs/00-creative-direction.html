Reference study

What actually makes each reference work

Fetched and read directly rather than assumed — text/structure/positioning verified, visual specifics (exact motion timing, precise hues) are informed inference where WebFetch can't see pixels. Not copied from below; each entry ends in the one lesson carried into the directions.

digitz.fr

Confident restraint: numbered sections (01–09) as real methodology, not decoration, and a deliberately tempered AI claim — "AI as new layer, not new métier." Monochrome palette lets the content carry the page.

Lesson: structural numbering only earns its place when it maps to something genuinely sequential — and restraint reads as more confident than hype.
trionn.com

Bold statement typography, a split-reveal treatment on "Designed to mean something," and a playful hold-to-interact micro-mechanic. High black/white/accent contrast does the work, not decoration volume.

Lesson: one memorable interaction mechanic, committed to fully, beats several timid ones.
delucks.com

A genuine precedent — a specialized agency positioning on "search engines and AI" specifically, blue accent, and copy built entirely around accountability: "no anonymous ticket systems, no rotating junior contacts."

Lesson: the boutique/accountable-senior-contact angle is a proven differentiator in exactly this category, not a hypothetical.
orionix.framer.website

Oversized headlines with deliberate line-break rhythm ("From Complexity to Clarity in 3 Steps"), strategy-before-execution positioning, reel-forward case-study presentation.

Lesson: a structural conceit ("complexity → clarity") stated once and then executed everywhere is stronger than restating the theme in every section's copy.
Three original directions

Each grounded in something only Boost Web Digital can say

Not three reskins of the same idea — each stakes out different territory: the healthcare subject itself, the actual product (rankings moving up), and the actual differentiator (honesty + AI-search extraction). Explained in full below; my recommendation is at the end, not withheld until then.

Direction A

Vital Signal

Treats search visibility as a vital sign to monitor — a single visual pun ties healthcare and SEO together literally: the same line reads as an ECG trace and a ranking-trend chart.

Live motif — waveform / trend-line

Design philosophy

Visibility monitored like a vital sign — calm authority, not marketing hype.

Visual identity

Hairline chart-grid, tick-mark dividers, monospace annotations beside real numbers.

Typography

Fraunces large and tight-tracked, like a single bold chart reading; Geist body with tabular-nums wherever digits appear.

Color usage

Accent reserved almost exclusively for the live line itself — never a decorative fill.

Layout principles

Strict horizontal-rule grid; sections divided by thin lines, not large gaps — a printout's structure.

Motion language

The waveform is ambient and continuous, amplitude tied to scroll position — always quietly running.

Signature interaction

The proof spotlight's real metric draws itself in as a live line, not a counting-up number.

Lighting & depth

Flat and clinical on purpose — depth comes from the grid receding, not soft ambient glow.

Scroll storytelling

Each section break is a brief flatline that resumes — "caught it, fixed it," literally the value prop.

Hero concept

One calm waveform spanning the hero width; the H1 sits on it like a chart annotation.

Homepage flow

Calm line → splits into one trace per specialty → spikes for the real case study → flat/healthy comparison → services → FAQ → CTA.

Differentiation: most healthcare-marketing sites default to generic corporate-blue or soft wellness aesthetics. Nobody else says "we monitor your visibility like a vital sign" this literally — and it's specifically credible because the client base is healthcare.

Direction B

Ascent

The product is "we move you up" — expressed architecturally through composition and light, not through the up-and-to-the-right stock chart every growth agency already uses.

Live motif — altitude glow (move cursor)

Design philosophy

Growth as spatial confidence — altitude and light, not an illustrated arrow.

Visual identity

Layered horizontal strata suggesting depth/atmosphere, like looking up through layers toward light.

Typography

Fraunces escalates in scale and confidence as the page progresses — quiet at the hero, largest at the CTA.

Color usage

The one accent hex, but its glow warms and brightens deeper into the page — altitude as light, not hue-shift.

Layout principles

Negative space sits above each section, not below — content rises into view rather than fades up from below.

Motion language

True parallax bands (GSAP + ScrollTrigger earns its place here) — background moves slower than foreground.

Signature interaction

A cursor-reactive altitude glow that brightens the further down the page you've scrolled.

Lighting & depth

The richest of the three — real layered Z-space and directional glow, kept as the one signature, not stacked with others.

Scroll storytelling

Each section is visibly higher/brighter/more confident than the last, peaking at the final CTA.

Hero concept

Type set small and grounded low in the viewport, vast space above it — "there's room to rise."

Homepage flow

Quiet grounded hero → first rise at specialty recognition → visible jump in scale/brightness at proof → services → FAQ → the brightest, largest final CTA.

Differentiation: "growth" in this category is almost always illustrated literally (a chart, an arrow). This expresses it as composition and atmosphere instead — the page itself performs the metaphor rather than depicting it.

Direction C

Signal & Noise

Healthcare marketing is full of identical, unverifiable claims (noise). Boost Web Digital's honesty rule and AI-search specialty are both, literally, about extracting real signal from noise — so the design system enforces honesty rather than just stating it.

Live motif — focus pull (move cursor)
results-driven Kaja Hair Studio data-backed industry-leading full-service

Design philosophy

Be the one real signal in a category full of noise — literally, not just in copy.

Visual identity

Unproven/aspirational content renders soft and blurred; anything real and verifiable renders crisp.

Typography

Fraunces headlines are always tack-sharp — never blurred — reinforcing that words here are trustworthy.

Color usage

The accent marks "this is real, look here" exclusively — an in-focus ring beside anything independently verifiable.

Layout principles

One sharp focal element per section, soft receded context around it — a depth-of-field composition, not flat uniform sharpness.

Motion language

A cursor-driven focus pull — nearby blurred elements sharpen slightly as the cursor approaches.

Signature interaction

The proof spotlight is the one section already in perfect focus at rest — no interaction needed to "earn" clarity, unlike the aspirational sections.

Lighting & depth

Photographic depth-of-field — aperture blur and bokeh-like glow, a more cinematic depth than flat glassmorphism.

Scroll storytelling

Scrolling from aspirational (softly blurred edges) into the one proof section (fully sharp) performs the honesty rule visually.

Hero concept

The real headline resolves into focus out of a field of blurred, illegible generic-agency phrases dissolving behind it.

Homepage flow

Noise dissolving to signal → specialties rendered at different focus levels based on real proof → the one fully-resolved proof section → services → FAQ → sharp, decisive CTA.

Differentiation: a competitor fabricating testimonials structurally could not adopt this direction without becoming honest first — it's the only one of the three that's defensible by policy, not just by aesthetics.
