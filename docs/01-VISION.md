# Vision

## Mission

Build the digital flagship of Boost Web Digital: a site that attracts, educates, and converts healthcare practices into clients for SEO, GEO, and marketing automation services.

## Goals

- Premium brand perception — the site is the proof of the agency's own competence
- Organic SEO / GEO growth as a lead channel
- Lead generation and conversion, not just brand awareness
- Scalable architecture that survives years of content growth
- Long-term maintainability by a non-programmer founder, then a small team

## Audience

Healthcare practices: med spas, dental, dermatology, hair restoration/transplant, plastic surgery, orthodontists.

## The honesty rule

**Kaja Hair Studio** (hair transplant & restoration) is the only real, nameable client with a genuine case study. Every other specialty on the site is aspirational positioning — the agency wants to serve them, but has no live proof yet.

This distinction must be preserved everywhere: no fabricated testimonials, logos, or results for specialties without a real client. Where proof doesn't exist yet, say so honestly (see `WhyChooseUs.tsx`'s honest proof block) rather than implying false credibility. Trust is the product; a single fabricated claim undermines it more than an honest "we're new to this vertical" ever would.
