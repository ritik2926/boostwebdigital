## Principle

The first screen is a single, complete statement — not a stack of sections racing to fit above the fold. The homepage itself is now complete end-to-end, Navbar through Footer; what's still open is everything *outside* it — the specialty hub pages, service pages, case studies, and the rest of the locked sitemap in `docs/13-URL-ARCHITECTURE.md`. See [10-ROADMAP.md](10-ROADMAP.md) for what's next.

Every section should lead naturally into the next — one continuous story, not stacked independent blocks. Favor pacing and narrative flow over adding more sections; a shorter page that reads as one story beats a longer page that reads as a checklist.
